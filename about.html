How a story makes the page

A memorable explanation is not enough on its own. Each entry has to survive three questions before it joins the collection.

01 · Trace

Where did the claim begin?

Find the earliest credible account, then separate a founder’s stated intent from a story repeated after the fact.

02 · Check

Can a reader verify it?

Prefer first-hand or institutional sources. If the evidence supports the meaning but not the naming decision, say only that.

03 · Tell

What makes it worth remembering?

Keep the human detail that gives the name texture, while leaving speculation and corporate mythology outside the frame.

Built to stay small

The site is deliberately static and dependency-free. It works without an account, stores only a visitor’s light-or-dark preference in their browser, and sends no analytics or tracking data anywhere.

The collection will grow when another name has both a surprising story and evidence strong enough to publish — not simply to make the page longer.
